⚡

Tier Conflict Detection — The Killer Feature

When laws at different levels contradict each other, Inveritas automatically flags the gap. A local ordinance can't create a lesser penalty than state law — when a municipal court charges under a local ordinance but the state statute has a more favorable provision, that's a defense vector most attorneys miss entirely.
