4. Case Data and Evidence Handling

Your case data and evidence files contain potentially sensitive legal information. We treat this data with heightened security measures:

  • All evidence files are stored in private, encrypted storage buckets accessible only to the uploading user;
  • SHA-256 cryptographic hashes are computed at upload time and verified on access to ensure data integrity;
  • An immutable chain-of-custody log records every access, download, and modification event for each evidence item;
  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforce strict tenant isolation — users can only access their own cases and evidence;
  • Case data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption;
  • Access to evidence files requires time-limited signed URLs that expire after one hour;
  • Analysis results are versioned and immutable — previous versions cannot be altered.

Important: Inveritas is not a law firm and cannot guarantee attorney-client privilege protection for data uploaded to the Service. You are responsible for determining what data to upload. If you are an attorney, consider whether uploading client data is consistent with your ethical obligations.

5. Data Security and Breach Notification

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit;
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for all database records and file storage;
  • Encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing via Supabase Auth);
  • Server-side API key management (credentials never exposed to the browser);
  • Input validation and prompt injection sanitization;
  • Rate limiting (5 requests/minute/IP on analysis endpoints);
  • Security headers: CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options DENY, X-XSS-Protection;
  • Row Level Security enforced on all database tables;
  • Regular security reviews and audit logging.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Breach Notification. In the event of a security breach that compromises your personal information, we will:
  • Notify affected individuals within 60 days of discovering the breach, as required by Texas Business & Commerce Code §521.053;
  • Notify the Texas Attorney General within 30 days if the breach affects 250 or more Texas residents;
  • Provide notification by email to the address associated with your account;
  • Include in our notification: a description of the breach, the type of information compromised, steps we are taking, and steps you can take to protect yourself.

7. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained for the life of your account plus 3 years after deletion;
  • Case Data and Evidence: Retained for the life of your account. Upon account deletion, case data is deleted within 30 days. Evidence files are permanently deleted from storage;
  • Analysis Data: Retained indefinitely for AI improvement and aggregate analytics. Upon account deletion, analysis results are anonymized;
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance;
  • Usage Logs and Audit Logs: Retained for 2 years;
  • Anonymized/Aggregated Data: Retained indefinitely.

9. Texas Residents (TDPSA)

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), effective July 1, 2024, provides Texas residents with specific data privacy rights. As a Texas-based company, we are committed to compliance with the TDPSA. Your rights under the TDPSA include:

  • Right to Know: You may confirm whether we are processing your personal data and access that data;
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data;
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal data you have provided or that we have obtained;
  • Right to Data Portability: You may obtain a copy of your personal data in a portable and readily usable format;
  • Right to Opt Out: You may opt out of the processing of personal data for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

To submit a TDPSA request, email privacy@inveritaslaw.com with "TDPSA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days.

Sensitive Data: Under the TDPSA, we will not process sensitive personal data (including health data, biometric data, or precise geolocation) without your express consent. The Service does not collect biometric data or precise geolocation. If you upload medical records as case evidence, you are providing express consent for us to store and process that data as described in this policy.

Appeal Process: If we decline your TDPSA request, you may appeal by emailing privacy@inveritaslaw.com with "TDPSA Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond within 60 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov.
