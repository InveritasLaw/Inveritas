Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 2, 2026  |  Effective Date: April 2, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Inveritas ("we," "us," "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit inveritaslaw.com (the "Service").

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide information to site owners, and enable certain features.

3. Categories of Cookies

Essential Cookies: Necessary for the Service to function. Includes authentication, session management, consent tracking, and payment processing. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the Service by collecting information about pages visited, time spent, and usage patterns.

Preference Cookies: Remember choices you make (such as jurisdiction selection) to provide a more personalized experience.

4. Other Tracking Technologies

  • Local Storage: Used by Supabase Auth to store authentication tokens;
  • Session Storage: Used to maintain state during your current browsing session;
  • Server-Side Logging: Records of API requests including IP address, user agent, and timestamps.

5. Your Choices

Consent Banner: When you first visit the Service, you will be presented with a consent banner. You must accept essential cookies and our Terms of Service to use the Service.

Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to control cookies through settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent the Service from functioning properly.

Opt-Out: You may opt out of analytics cookies through the consent banner. Essential cookies cannot be disabled while using the Service.

6. Third-Party Cookies

  • Stripe: Payment processing and fraud prevention;
  • Supabase: Authentication and session cookies;
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (may set performance cookies).

We do not control third-party cookies. Please review the privacy policies of these services for more information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about cookies? Contact us at privacy@inveritaslaw.com.
